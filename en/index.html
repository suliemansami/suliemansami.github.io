The company works. Still, it cannot be moved: the capital does not come in, the buyer hesitates, and nobody says why. The cause is almost always the same. The company was built to be run, not to be valued.

Three pillars. It is rarely just one of them that pinches.

01 FINANCING

The right capital, in the right order

Capital structure, investor readiness, term sheets and M&A. Sometimes the next step is equity, sometimes debt, sometimes a strategic partner or a structured exit. The job is to choose correctly and get the deal across the line.

02 BUSINESS MODEL

The version of the company that can be funded

How the company makes money, who it sells to, and what has to be true for the model to scale. The model is pressure-tested before it meets investors, not after.

03 COMMERCIALIZATION

From a good product to real revenue

Go-to-market, pricing, partnerships and the first serious sales motion. Partner-led distribution comes up often, especially for companies that cannot afford a large sales organization from day one.

Selected work

AI company ahead of listingAdvisor and capital raiser, Karman Advisory. 2026.

Secured a USD 60 million financing commitment ahead of the company's listing on Nasdaq Nordic. The mandate covered structure, investor dialogue and the path to a listed market. Read more on samisulieman.org.

Fintech company to a public listingFounder and largest shareholder, Plexian. 2017 to 2021.

I founded Plexian in Malmö in 2017. In April 2021 the company listed on Nasdaq First North Growth Market after an oversubscribed SEK 38 million issue, and I was its largest shareholder at the time. The company was valued at SEK 33 million before the issue. Seven months later its market capitalisation was around SEK 250 million.

Sale of patented dental technologyOwner and M&A transaction lead, Jaws. Ongoing.

I lead the sale of Jaws, a patented technology for implant prosthetics with an international patent portfolio. The sellers are represented by Karman Advisory and the process is ongoing.

Banking venture in LondonChairman, City of London Capital. 2016.

I established City of London Capital in London as chairman, with Professor Moorad Choudhry as CEO. The same year I met Lithuania's finance minister and his staff on EU licences for financial companies after Brexit, and presented the venture to around 1,500 investors and financial companies in Shenzhen.

Staffing company from zeroFounder and owner. Malmö, 2008 to 2010.

My staffing company had 60 employees and five per cent of the local market after twelve months. It handled half of the electricity meter replacements in the City of Malmö and its neighbouring municipalities when the legal requirement came into force.

Income insurance for business ownersProcurement and placement, SMÅA. 2012 to 2013.

I procured and placed an income insurance for the members of SMÅA, the Swedish unemployment fund for business owners. The cover sat on top of the unemployment fund and was one of Sweden's largest group insurances. Several had tried for years without getting such a solution in place.

How I work

Diagnosis

A first conversation about the situation, the cause and what waiting costs. Three sentences are often enough. If I am the wrong kind of advisor for you, I say so and point you to the right one.

Structure

We choose the path: capital, business model, commercial setup, or all three. The time window is defined. Everyone knows what must have happened and by when.

Execution

I work closely with the owner until the deal is done. No process for the sake of process. No urgency that is not real.
